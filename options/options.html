CX Debug Assistant

Bring-your-own-key. There is no Unbxd backend in this version — analysis requests go straight from your browser to Google's Gemini (Vertex AI or the Gemini API), using your key.

Gemini

Capture & prompt

What gets sent

  • Request/response metadata: URL with secret query params redacted, method, status, timing, and a safe subset of headers.
  • Console errors and warnings, truncated.
  • For alignment issues: box models and a fixed list of computed style properties.
  • For SRP/PLP and autosuggest-data issues: request params plus response counts and field names — never the catalogue data.
  • Never: response bodies, cookies, Authorization / x-api-key headers, or anything from tabs other than the one you captured.
